Privacy Policy — VeriCall

Effective Date: March 2026

1. Overview

CallSealSkr ("the App") is published by MidMightBit Games, based in Melbourne, VIC, Australia. The App is a communication verification tool for the Solana Seeker device. It monitors phone calls and SMS messages, creates SHA-256 hashes of communication metadata, and records those hashes on the Solana blockchain as immutable proof that a communication occurred.

CallSealSkr is built with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect personal data, we do not use analytics or tracking services, we do not store data in the cloud, and we do not serve advertisements. All data remains on your device.

Critical Privacy Commitment: CallSealSkr does NOT record, store, or transmit the actual content of any phone call or SMS message. No call audio is captured. No message text is read. Only communication metadata (phone number, timestamp, duration, direction) is processed, and only as input to a one-way SHA-256 hash function. The resulting hash cannot be reversed to recover the original metadata.

2. Legal Basis for Data Processing

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR, the legal basis for processing your wallet public address (if you choose to connect a wallet) is legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) — specifically, the legitimate interest in providing SGT verification and hash recording access that you voluntarily opt into by connecting your wallet. This processing is minimal, expected by the user, and proportionate.

The legal basis for processing communication metadata to generate hashes is consent (Article 6(1)(a)) — you explicitly enable call and SMS monitoring within the App, and you can disable it at any time.

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), the wallet public address is collected in accordance with APP 3 (collection of solicited personal information) for the primary purpose of on-chain identity verification and feature access. Communication metadata processing is covered by APP 3 as information collected for the primary purpose of hash generation at your request.

3. How Hashing Works

When you enable communication monitoring, CallSealSkr processes metadata as follows:

  • Communication metadata (phone number, timestamp, duration, direction) is collected on-device
  • A SHA-256 hash is computed locally on your device from this metadata
  • The hash is a fixed-length, irreversible string — the original metadata cannot be reconstructed from the hash
  • Only the hash is submitted to the Solana blockchain via the Memo Program
  • The raw metadata is discarded from memory immediately after hashing

At no point does any raw communication metadata leave your device. Only the cryptographic hash is recorded on-chain.

4. Data We Store Locally

Communication Hashes (Local Only)

  • SHA-256 hashes of communication metadata
  • On-chain transaction signatures for recorded hashes
  • Hash recording status (pending, confirmed, failed)
  • Stored in EncryptedSharedPreferences on your device

App Settings (Local Only)

  • Call monitoring enabled/disabled preference
  • SMS monitoring enabled/disabled preference
  • Auto-hash preference (automatic vs. manual hash recording)
  • Notification preferences
  • Stored in standard SharedPreferences on your device

Wallet Public Address (Optional, Encrypted)

  • If you connect a Solana wallet, your public address is stored locally on your device
  • Encrypted using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256-GCM for values, AES-256-SIV for keys), backed by the Android Keystore
  • Used solely for SGT verification and hash recording payments
  • This is a public key only — we never request, access, or store your private keys

5. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Call audio or voice recordings
  • SMS or message content (text body)
  • Raw communication metadata (phone numbers, contact names)
  • Personal information (name, email, address)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Browsing history

  • Private keys or seed phrases
  • Payment card details or bank information
  • Cookies or similar tracking technologies

6. Phone and SMS Permissions

CallSealSkr requires the following permissions to function:

  • READ_PHONE_STATE — to detect incoming and outgoing calls and obtain call metadata (phone number, duration, direction)
  • READ_CALL_LOG — to access call history metadata for hash generation
  • RECEIVE_SMS — to detect incoming SMS messages and obtain metadata (sender number, timestamp)
  • READ_SMS — to access SMS metadata for hash generation. The App reads only metadata fields (sender, timestamp, type). Message body content is never read, processed, or stored.

Important: These permissions are used exclusively to extract metadata for SHA-256 hashing. The App does not record call audio, read message content, or transmit any raw communication data. All metadata is processed locally, hashed immediately, and the raw data is discarded from memory.

7. Wallet Connection (Optional)

CallSealSkr supports optional wallet connection via Sign In With Solana (SIWS) using the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA). Wallet connection enables:

  • SGT Verification: Checking on-chain ownership of a Seeker Genesis Token to unlock free hash recording
  • Hash Recording Payments: Processing SOL or SKR payments for hash recording (for non-SGT holders)

The MWA protocol operates locally between apps on your device. No wallet data is transmitted to external servers by MidMightBit Games. We store only your wallet public address (encrypted). We never have access to your private keys or the ability to sign transactions on your behalf without your explicit approval in your wallet app.

8. Payments and Transactions

CallSealSkr offers hash recording that can be paid for with SOL (0.01 per hash) or SKR tokens (0.01 per hash). SGT holders record hashes for free. These transactions are processed entirely on the Solana blockchain:

  • You approve each transaction individually in your wallet app
  • Transaction details are recorded on the public Solana blockchain
  • MidMightBit Games does not store payment details, transaction history, or financial records on our servers
  • We do not process credit cards, bank transfers, or fiat currency

9. Network Requests

The App makes the following network requests:

  • Solana RPC Endpoint: To verify SGT ownership on-chain and submit hash memo transactions. Your wallet public address is sent as part of the query. This is publicly available blockchain data.
  • Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA): Local device-to-device communication between CallSealSkr and your wallet app. This does not leave your device.

All network communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. The App enforces HTTPS for all connections and does not permit cleartext traffic.

10. IP Address Disclosure

The App does not collect or store your IP address. However, when the App makes network requests to Solana RPC endpoints, your IP address is transmitted to those services as part of standard internet protocol. MidMightBit Games has no access to any logs or data collected by third-party RPC providers. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

11. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device. No data is transmitted to any server owned or operated by MidMightBit Games. Security measures include:

  • Wallet public address encrypted with Android EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256)
  • Communication hashes encrypted with Android EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256)
  • Encryption keys secured by the Android Keystore
  • HTTPS enforced for all network connections
  • No cloud storage, no server-side databases, no remote backups
  • allowBackup is set to false in the App manifest to prevent wallet and hash data from being included in device backups

12. No Accounts Created

CallSealSkr does not create user accounts. There is no registration, no login system, and no user profiles. Wallet connection via SIWS is optional and used solely for SGT verification and hash recording payments. Disconnecting your wallet removes all wallet-related data from your device.

13. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party libraries:

  • Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter: Used to connect to your Solana wallet app on-device. No data is sent to Solana Mobile.

The App does NOT include any:

  • Analytics SDKs
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Crash reporting SDKs
  • Social media SDKs
  • Push notification services
  • Cloud storage services

14. Data Retention

Your data is retained locally on your device for as long as the App is installed. Data is automatically deleted when you:

  • Disconnect your wallet within the App
  • Clear the App's data through Android Settings > Apps > CallSealSkr > Clear Data
  • Uninstall the App

Communication hashes, app settings, and wallet data are removed when the App is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

Note: SHA-256 hashes that have been recorded on the Solana blockchain are immutable and cannot be deleted. This is an inherent characteristic of blockchain technology. However, these on-chain hashes cannot be reversed to recover the original communication metadata.

15. Cross-Border Data Transfers

When making RPC calls, your wallet public address and IP address are transmitted to Solana RPC endpoints to retrieve on-chain data and submit hash transactions. Your wallet public address is already publicly visible on the Solana blockchain. No user-specific data is sent to MidMightBit Games servers (we have none).

We rely on the public nature of blockchain data and your explicit request to use this service as the basis for these transmissions. For EU/UK users, this processing is necessary for the performance of the service at your request (GDPR Article 49(1)(b)).

16. Your Rights

Right to Delete (Erasure)

You can delete all App data at any time by:

  1. Disconnecting your wallet within the App to remove wallet data
  2. Going to Android Settings > Apps > CallSealSkr > Clear Data to remove all local data
  3. Uninstalling the App

This satisfies the right to erasure under GDPR Article 17, UK GDPR, and the Australian Privacy Act. Note that on-chain hashes cannot be deleted due to the immutable nature of blockchain technology, but they contain no personally identifiable information.

Right to Access

All data stored by the App is visible within the App itself (hash history, app settings, and wallet connection status). You may also contact us to request a summary of any data associated with your wallet address.

Right to Rectification (GDPR Article 16)

As the only externally-sourced data is your wallet public address from the Solana blockchain, the App displays factual on-chain data. If you believe any data is inaccurate, you can disconnect and reconnect your wallet to refresh on-chain data.

Right to Restriction of Processing (GDPR Article 18)

You may restrict the processing of your wallet address at any time by disconnecting your wallet. You may disable call and SMS monitoring at any time to stop metadata processing. This stops all data processing and removes your wallet address from local storage. You may reconnect at any time to resume the service.

Right to Object

You may object to the processing of your wallet address at any time by disconnecting your wallet, which removes your wallet address from local storage. You may object to communication metadata processing by disabling monitoring in the App settings.

Right to Portability

As all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data at all times. Your wallet public address is the only externally-sourced data element and is already in your possession.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority:

  • Australia: Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) — oaic.gov.au
  • EU/EEA: Your local Data Protection Authority
  • UK: Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) — ico.org.uk
