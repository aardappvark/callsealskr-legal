2. Description of Service

CallSealSkr is a communication verification application that monitors phone calls and SMS messages, creates SHA-256 hashes of communication metadata, and records those hashes on the Solana blockchain as immutable proof that a communication occurred.

Key features include:

  • SHA-256 hashing of communication metadata (phone number, timestamp, duration, direction)
  • On-chain hash recording via the Solana Memo Program
  • Communication verification history with on-chain transaction references
  • Optional wallet connection for Seeker platform identity and SGT verification
  • Free hash recording for SGT holders; SOL or SKR payment for others
  • Restoring a paid SKR Premium unlock from the blockchain on a new install or device

The App does NOT record call audio and does NOT transmit the content of any call or message. Only metadata hashes are created and sent on-chain. The App does keep a local log of the events it observes, which includes the other party's phone number and, while SMS previews are enabled in Settings, the first 50 characters of a message. That log stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

3. Nature of Communication Hashes

You acknowledge and agree that:

  • SHA-256 hashes are one-way cryptographic functions — the original metadata cannot be reconstructed from the hash
  • Hashes recorded on the Solana blockchain are immutable and permanent — they cannot be edited, deleted, or removed once confirmed on-chain
  • On-chain hashes serve as proof that a communication occurred at a specific time, but they are not legal evidence in any jurisdiction unless specifically accepted as such by the relevant authority
  • The accuracy of hashes depends on the accuracy of the metadata provided by your device's operating system
  • Hash recording is subject to Solana network availability and confirmation times
