What is CallSealSkr?

CallSealSkr is a communication verification dApp for the Solana Seeker phone. It monitors phone calls, creates SHA-256 hashes of call metadata (timestamp, duration, direction and a device identifier), and records these hashes on the Solana blockchain as immutable proof that a call occurred.

Important: CallSealSkr does not record call audio and does not transmit the content of any call or message. Only metadata hashes go on-chain. A local, on-device log keeps only the type of call, its time and its duration — never the other party's number, and the App has no access to your messages. See the Privacy Policy.

Features

Metadata Hashing

Creates SHA-256 hashes of call metadata — timestamp, duration, direction and a device identifier — never the actual content.

Blockchain Proof

Records hashes on the Solana blockchain via the Memo Program as immutable, timestamped proof that a communication occurred.

SGT Free Access

Seeker Genesis Token (SGT) holders get free hash recording. Others pay 0.063 SOL or 660 SKR per hash, or 660 SKR once for SKR Premium.

Seeker Integration

Sign In With Solana, Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA), and native Seeker device integration.

Important Notice

CallSealSkr creates cryptographic proof that a communication occurred. It does not record call audio and never sends your metadata or message text anywhere. Only SHA-256 hashes of metadata go on-chain, and those hashes are irreversible — the original metadata cannot be reconstructed from the hash. On-chain hashes are immutable and cannot be deleted once recorded.
